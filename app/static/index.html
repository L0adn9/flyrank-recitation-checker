Recite

Recite from memory. See every word you missed.

Record yourself reciting a passage. Recite listens, checks it against the Quran text word by word, and tells you what to revise and when.

  1. Pick a passage and recite it with the text hidden.
  2. Get it checked in seconds: skipped, wrong, repeated and out-of-order words are marked.
  3. Revise what is slipping. Each morning brings a short plan built from your own mistakes.

Your recordings stay on the server that runs Recite. Speech recognition runs locally; nothing is sent to an outside service.
